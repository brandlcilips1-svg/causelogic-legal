Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

CauseLogic Autopilot ("the Application") is an internal content creation and scheduling tool operated exclusively by the owner of the CauseLogic channel.

Data Access & Usage

The Application accesses the YouTube Data API v3 solely to upload, update metadata, and attach thumbnails to videos owned by the authorized channel creator. The Application does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data from external users or third parties.

Data Storage & Revocation

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the creator's private machine solely to authenticate scheduled uploads. Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account Permissions.
